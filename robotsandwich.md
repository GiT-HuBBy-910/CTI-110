# Sandwich Construction

## Assumption

- We have a clear and clean countertop
- On the counter we have: 
    - A plate
    - Two slices of bread lying side-by-side left and right
    - Closed jar of PB
    - Closed jar of Jelly
    - A butter knife
    - 2 Napkins
    - Trash can at the end of counter on floor
    - The robot is charged and ready to go
  

## Steps to Build The Sandwich
1. Pick up PB jar with left hand with lid facing up
2. With right hand grab PB lid firm twist CCW remove lid
3. Place PB lid on countertop
4. Pick up knife handle with right hand
5. Use knife to scoop 1 tablespoon PB
6. Spread PB onto right bread slice same thickness across
7. Sit PB jar on counter
8. Grab napkin with left hand and clean knife
9. Dispose of napkin in trash can
10. Return to counter
11. Sit knife on counter
12. Pick up PB jar with left hand open end up
13. Grab PB lid with right hand
14. Place PB lid on PB Jar and tighten CW firm
15. Place PB jar on counter
16. Pick up Jelly jar with left hand lid facing up
17. With right hand grab Jelly lid firm twist CCW remove lid
18. Place Jelly lid on countertop
19. Pick up knife handle with right hand
20. Use knife to scoop 1 tablespoon Jelly
21. Spread Jelly onto left bread slice same thickness across
22. Sit Jelly jar on counter
23. Grab napkin with left hand and clean knife
24. Dispose of napkin in trash can
25. Return to counter
26. Sit knife on counter
27. Pick up Jelly jar with left hand open end up
28. Grab Jelly lid with right hand
29. Place Jelly lid on Jelly jar and tighten CW firm
30. Place Jelly jar on counter
31. Gently pick up PB bread slice from one edge with right hand
32. Place PB slice on Jelly slice with PB and Jelly touching with edges even
33. Put PB in pantry
34. Put Jelly in refrigerator 
35. Put knife in sink
36. Place PB Jelly sandwich on plate centered



## Exit Conditions
- Plate and constructed sandwich are the only items on the counter
